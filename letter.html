Happy Monthsary, my Jeniesys. ♡

Hi, my love. 🥹❤️

If I could fold all the love I have for you into this little page and hand it to you like a letter, I would. It still wouldn’t hold everything, but I hope it gives you a small glimpse of what you mean to me.

Thank you for the memories we already share and for the little moments we haven’t made yet. I don’t expect us to be perfect. I hope we keep learning each other, listening with patience, being honest, apologizing when we need to, and showing our love through the small things we do.

I want more random laughs, more photos we’ll look at later, more silly conversations, and more days where we remind each other that we’re on the same team. When life gets busy or a day doesn’t go the way we hoped, I hope we still make room for kindness, understanding, and a little extra patience.

And if you ever wonder whether you’re special to me, let this be your reminder: you are. Not because every moment is perfect, but because you’re you. Thank you for being part of my life, Jeniesys. I’m grateful for this chapter with you and excited for the pages we’ll write next.

With all my heart, today and in all our little tomorrows. ♡
